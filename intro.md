# Physical Hydrology

An online textbook for the graduate Physical Hydrology course in the Department of Civil Engineering, Jeonbuk National University, by [Daeha Kim](https://hydroclimatol.com/).

```{admonition} Work in progress
:class: note
The chapters are being written. The table of contents shows the planned structure of the book.
```

The book follows water through the atmosphere, across the land surface and below it, and back to the stream. Each process is derived from the conservation of mass, momentum or energy, simplified step by step, and finally written in the lumped form that is used at the scale of a catchment. Along the way the book asks why such simple descriptions work as well as they do, and where they fail.

Readers are expected to know undergraduate hydrology and fluid mechanics, as covered in the companion books [Engineering Hydrology](https://hydroclimatol.com/engineering-hydrology/) and [Hydraulics](https://hydroclimatol.com/hydraulics/). Frequency analysis is covered in a separate textbook on statistical hydrology, and the people behind the theories in [*A Brief History of Fluid Mechanics and Water Sciences*](https://hydroclimatol.com/water-history/).

Figures and tables are generated by the Python code that accompanies them, which runs every time the book is built. Worked examples use invented numbers or synthetic data. Each chapter ends with a lab, a Colab notebook that applies the theory to real, openly available data.

The whole book is also available as a [PDF](https://hydroclimatol.com/physical-hydrology/physical-hydrology.pdf).
